# Neptune customer policy — approved by owner September 28, 2026

## Subscription terms
Today’s water-quality dashboard is free without an account. Neptune Pro costs $5/month or $40/year, depending on the plan selected at checkout, and renews automatically until canceled. Cancel renewal through billing settings; access continues through the paid period. Canceling renewal does not itself request a refund.

Full refund within 7 days of the first Neptune Pro purchase. Request it from your checkout email at ethan@projectneptune.co. This first-purchase guarantee does not apply to renewals.

Forecasts are estimates, not current lab results or official advisories. Always follow official beach advisories. Contact ethan@projectneptune.co for support.

## Privacy notice
You can view today’s water quality without an account. For Pro, Neptune uses your checkout email, account identifier, subscription status, billing-period information, and payment-provider identifiers to provide access. Following beaches stores your email, selected beaches, and alert-delivery records.

Stripe processes payment and billing details; Neptune stores subscription references rather than full card numbers. Clerk manages authentication, Neon hosts the database, Google Workspace/Gmail sends emails, and Vercel hosts the dashboard and provides analytics. PostHog provides usage analytics and error reporting.

Authentication uses cookies. Analytics and operational logs may include pages visited, interactions, browser/device details, and request information to understand use and investigate errors. Browser privacy settings may restrict cookies or analytics.

Manage alert choices on the dashboard or unsubscribe through an alert email. Contact ethan@projectneptune.co to request access, correction, or deletion of your information. Identity verification may be needed. Billing and operational records may be retained for transactions, disputes, or legal obligations. Canceling a subscription does not automatically delete your account.
